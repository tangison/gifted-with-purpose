# PRODUCT.md

## What this is

A mobile-first marketing and catalogue website for **Gifted with Purpose**, the trading name of
**Geneveve Gift Shop**, a Namibian mother and daughter business making personalised gifts.

Live: https://gifted-with-purpose.vercel.app
Repository: https://github.com/tangison/gifted-with-purpose (private)

## Who it is for

Namibian and South African buyers looking for a personalised gift, arriving on a phone from an
Instagram, Facebook or WhatsApp link. Mostly English, with an Afrikaans scripture line. They are
buying for someone specific, usually for an occasion, and they expect to talk to a person.

## The objective

Give the catalogue a permanent home outside Instagram stories and WhatsApp statuses, and make the
distance between "I like that" and "I have messaged to order" as close to one tap as possible.

## Primary conversion

**A WhatsApp order enquiry.** Every product button opens WhatsApp with the product name, size and
price already written. The gift bag does the same for several items in one message.

There is no cart checkout, no payment gateway and no user accounts. This is the model evidenced in
every asset the client supplied: the only call to action on their own product ads is a phone number.

## What the site contains

27 products across 5 collections. 11 carry a price read from the client's own ad artwork. The other
16 show "Price on request" because no price exists in any supplied asset.

18 public routes: home, 5 collections, our story, how to order, FAQ, contact, brand guide, human
sitemap, 3 legal pages, plus robots, XML sitemap, manifest and a real 404.

## What is deliberately absent

No testimonials, review counts or star ratings. No delivery costs or timeframes. No returns policy
text. No stock levels. No invented prices. No AI-generated product photography. No analytics,
cookies or third-party scripts. Each of these is absent because it was not supplied and inventing
it would misrepresent the business.
